Privacy Policy for Nebula Art

Last updated: May 29, 2026

Introduction

Nebula Art ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our iOS application.

Information We Collect

Nebula Art does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. We do not collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Device identifiers or location data
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Photos or media files from your device
  • Any user-generated content

Subscription Payments

Nebula Art offers an optional auto-renewable subscription with a 1-day free trial. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment card details, billing address, or any financial information. Apple's privacy policy applies to all payment-related data: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Permissions

The app may request the following permissions:

  • Camera / Microphone: Used solely for screen recording of particle animations you create. No audio or video is recorded without your explicit action.
  • Photo Library: Used only when you choose to save a screenshot of your particle animation to your device. No photos are accessed or uploaded.

All media files remain on your device. We never upload, share, or transmit any media.

Data Storage

All content you create within Nebula Art (particle animations, text inputs, screenshots, recordings) is stored locally on your device only. We do not maintain any servers or databases that store user data.

Third-Party Services

Nebula Art does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. We do not use any SDKs that track user behavior.

Children's Privacy

Nebula Art is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from children. Since we collect no personal data, our app is safe for users of any age.

Data Retention

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no data retention period to specify. All your content stays on your device and is under your full control.

Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no data to access, modify, or delete from our systems. All your data resides on your device and can be managed through your device settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
